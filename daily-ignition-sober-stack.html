Read this first. Stopping alcohol after heavy or daily drinking can be medically dangerous. If there is confusion, a seizure, hallucinations, a very fast heartbeat, or high fever after the last drink, call 911. If drinking has been daily, or there is any past history of shakes, seizures, or DTs when stopping, talk to a doctor about a supervised taper or medical detox before quitting outright. This app supports the recovery process. It is not a substitute for medical care.
